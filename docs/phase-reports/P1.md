## Security/privacy review

P1 handles adult account information only. No child records or guardian-child access are introduced. UI role switching remains confined to synthetic preview data. Runtime DB roles cannot own/bypass domain RLS; owner-only definer functions are explicitly documented instead of applying FORCE RLS recursively. Role-change auditing is in PostgreSQL and does not provision a paid log service. Scheduled backups, central logs and alerts remain inactive scaffolding.

## Known limitations / risks

- Not a production deployment. No live email, Azure resource configuration, legal approval or restore capability is claimed.
- Email is synchronous in P1; durable delivery/outbox retries remain P7. A provider accepting mail before a later transaction failure can leave an unusable link.
- Lost authenticator plus lost recovery codes needs a future audited operator recovery procedure. No public MFA reset bypass exists.
- P2 guardian-child registration/consent and P3 team CRUD/temporal rosters are not implemented. Preview matches are fictional.
- Actual third-party email/identity exceptions, deployment storage protection and regional SKU costs require later verification. Automated accessibility checks are not full certification.
